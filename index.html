About

How I work

I like problems where the robot can't directly measure the thing it needs. Scout has no wheel encoders, so it gets its motion from lidar. The fleet can't share maps out of radio range, so it has to learn when to come back.

I measure before I change things. When a sim disagrees with reality, I write the logger or the replay that shows exactly where, then fix the cause. Each project above has a list of what broke and how I found it.
